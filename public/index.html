About n8n-AI

n8n-AI is an automation companion for n8n workflows. It helps schedule and monitor tasks (such as database backups and notifications) and can integrate with third-party services you connect.

Built on top of the open-source n8n workflow automation platform, n8n-AI extends its capabilities with AI-assisted task management, so your automations run reliably around the clock without manual intervention.

How we use your Google account

We only request Google account access when you explicitly connect a Google service (e.g. Google Sheets, Google Drive, Gmail) to a workflow you configure. That access is used solely to perform the specific actions defined in your workflow — we do not use it for any other purpose.

Your credentials are stored securely and are never shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page .
