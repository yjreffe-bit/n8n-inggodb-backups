Privacy Policy

n8n-AI  •  Last updated: May 2025

1. Overview

n8n-AI ("we", "us", or "our") is a workflow automation service built on the n8n platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. By using n8n-AI you agree to the practices described here.

2. Data we collect

  • Account information: email address and display name you provide when you register or sign in.
  • Workflow data: the automation workflows you create and the configuration you supply (node settings, credentials references, etc.).
  • Execution logs: logs generated when workflows run, including input/output data you configure those workflows to handle.
  • Usage data: access timestamps, IP addresses, and browser/device information collected automatically via server logs.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the n8n-AI service.
  • To authenticate you and protect your account.
  • To execute the workflows you configure.
  • To diagnose errors and improve service reliability.
  • To respond to support inquiries.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

4. Google API data use

n8n-AI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

We request Google account access only when you connect a Google service (such as Google Sheets, Google Drive, or Gmail) to a workflow. Access tokens are used exclusively to perform the actions defined in your workflow. We do not use Google user data for advertising, to train AI/ML models, or to transfer data to third parties except as necessary to provide the specific functionality you request.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: infrastructure providers (e.g. hosting, database) that process data on our behalf under appropriate data-processing agreements.
  • Legal compliance: if required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with appropriate notice to you.

6. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Execution logs are retained for a rolling period (typically 30 days) unless you configure a longer retention period. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. No system is perfectly secure; if you suspect unauthorised access, please contact us immediately.

8. Your choices

  • Access & correction: you may review or update your account information at any time within the application.
  • Revoke Google access: visit your Google Account permissions page to revoke our access to your Google data at any time.
  • Account deletion: contact us to request deletion of your account and all associated data.
