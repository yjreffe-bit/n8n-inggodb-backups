Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

ADA Automation Hub uses Google OAuth solely to perform actions that you explicitly authorize. We do not store, sell, or share your personal data.

Information We Access

Depending on your permissions, ADA may access limited Google account data such as:

  • Your email address (for identifying your account)
  • Google Sheets you authorize
  • YouTube upload permissions (if enabled)

How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used only to execute the workflows you configure. No information is stored outside your n8n instance.

Data Storage

ADA does not store OAuth tokens, personal data, or workflow content on external servers. All data remains within your n8n environment.

Third‑Party Sharing

We do not share your data with any third parties.

Contact

If you have questions, contact the administrator of your ADA instance.
